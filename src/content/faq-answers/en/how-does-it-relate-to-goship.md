**Bio-GO-SHIP adds biological measurements to GO-SHIP hydrographic sections.**

GO-SHIP repeats ocean transects to track heat, carbon, and other physical and chemical properties. Bio-GO-SHIP coordinates plankton sampling and related measurements on those same lines so biology can be compared with the physical and chemical context.
